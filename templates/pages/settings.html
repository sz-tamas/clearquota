Clear local data

These actions permanently remove data from this local database.

Clear usage history

Removes usage snapshots, monthly summaries, and OpenAI cost and activity records. Projects, providers, credit events, and refresh logs remain.

Clear refresh logs

Removes the stored history of provider refresh attempts. Projects, providers, and usage history remain.

Erase all local app data

Removes every saved project, provider configuration, usage record, credit event, refresh log, and onboarding state. ClearQuota will open without a configured project. This does not delete secrets in Google Secret Manager.
